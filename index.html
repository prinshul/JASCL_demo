Continual Segmentation under Joint Nonstationarity

Real segmentation models face new classes, new domains and very few labels all at once. JASCL learns under this joint shift with two parts. Gradient-adaptive stabilization (GAS) perturbs each classifier weight with noise scaled by its gradient, so few-shot updates don't overwrite what was learned before. Prototype anchored supervision (PAS) uses unlabeled images, keeping a pseudo-label only when it is confident and close to its class prototype. Across medical and driving benchmarks, JASCL consistently improves over prior methods.
